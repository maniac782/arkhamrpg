How it works

Arkham Horror RPG Ledger keeps your group's Arkham Horror RPG investigators in one place. Every change shows up for everyone at the table straight away, on phones, tablets and laptops.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with Google, or with an email and password.
  2. Pick a username. It's the name other players see. Your email is never shown to them.
  3. Start a campaign or join one. Click + New campaign to start your own, or open the invite link a friend sent you. Invites sent to your email arrive in your inbox and wait on your My campaigns page.
  4. Make your investigator. Open the campaign and use the New investigator tab to build one step by step, or add a blank sheet and fill it in yourself.

Trying it without an account

The welcome screen has a sample campaign with an invented party: open sheets, spend dice and try the GM tab. Nothing you do there is saved.

To start your own, choose Start a campaign, no account needed. You get one campaign of your own where you're the GM, so you can make investigators, run a fight and try the Journal.

  • It's saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent to the site. Another browser or device won't see it, and clearing your browser's data (or private browsing) deletes it.
  • Keep it: tap Save to an account at the top of the campaign, then sign in or create an account. Everything is copied to your account, where you can invite your group, and the copy in the browser is removed.
  • If you're already signed in on that browser, My campaigns shows it with Save to my account, Open and Delete.
  • Invites, reminders, notifications and the Apple Calendar link need an account.

Who can do what

  • Owner: whoever started the campaign. Invites players, chooses the GM, sets house rules and the campaign picture, assigns investigators to players, and can remove members or delete the campaign. By default the owner can only change their own investigator; ticking Let me edit every investigator in Settings lets them change anyone's.
  • GM: chosen by the owner (the owner can be the GM). Gets the GM tab, and can award XP, start sessions and refill pools for everyone. Only the GM can see GM notes and enemies or clues that haven't been revealed.
  • Player: makes or claims one investigator and is the only one who can change it, apart from the GM's session tools.

Inviting players

Owners invite people from the campaign's Settings page, right after creating a campaign, or any time later:

  • Invite link: copy it into your group chat. Anyone with the link can join after signing in. Make a new link stops the old one from working.
  • By email: the site emails them the invite, and it waits on their My campaigns page when they sign in with that address.

A campaign can have up to 12 members.

Inside a campaign

Party

Everyone's investigators at a glance: dice pool, horror, insight and injuries. Tap one to open their sheet.

Investigator sheets

Tap a portrait, the campaign picture next to the campaign's name, or a handout to see it full size. Spend and refill dice, take damage and horror, record injuries and traumas, manage skills, knacks, weapons, gear and money, and spend XP. The Recent list shows what changed. Open printable sheet gives a print-ready copy (print on Letter paper with background graphics on); Back returns to the ledger. In the installed app on iPhone or iPad, Print opens the sheet in Safari (iOS 17 or later) and brings up the print screen, because the installed app can’t print.

New investigator

Builds a character following the Core Rulebook's creation rules, with the gear budget and XP worked out as you go. If you stop halfway, it's kept on your device for that campaign for two weeks; Start over clears it.

Next session

The owner (in the campaign's Settings) or the GM (on the GM tab) sets the date and time of the next game, where it is, and optionally how long it should run. Start typing an address in Where to pick from suggestions. Then:

  • Countdown: everyone sees it on the campaign's card and at the top of the Party tab.
  • Add to calendar: Google Calendar, Apple Calendar (iPhone or Mac), Outlook, or a calendar file for anything else.
  • Directions and food: if the place is an address, tap it for Directions or Food nearby in Google Maps.
  • Reminder: the day before, everyone gets an email, plus a notification on devices where they've turned those on. Anyone can turn reminders off under Your account.

Journal

Clues and handouts the GM has revealed, enemies the GM is showing, the campaign tracker (in-game date, open threads, people met and places), and Past sessions: a recap of each finished session, with the GM's summary on top if they wrote one.

For the GM

  • Next session: set the date, time, length and place at the top of the GM tab (see Next session above).
  • Scenes and turns: name the scene, start a fight (with an optional surprise round) and step through turns. Players see the scene and whose turn it is on their sheets.
  • Encounters: add enemies from the Core Rulebook's profiles or your own, track their pools, injuries and traumas, and choose when players can see them. Only the GM sees an enemy's profile. The book's profiles aren't offered in the sample campaign or a campaign saved only in your browser.
  • Clues and handouts: write one, attach a picture, and reveal it to everyone or to one player. Players with notifications on get one when you reveal a clue to them.
  • End of session: award XP to everyone at once and mark momentous sessions. Starting the next session refills insight and begins a new recap.
  • Recaps: each session's story and everyone's activity are collected automatically. Add an optional summary for players; they see it in the Journal once the session is over.
  • Private tools: GM notes, a hidden dice roller and a log of what you've done.

Your account

Click your icon at the top right and choose Your account to upload a profile photo, pick a colour for your initial, or change your reminders and notifications. The same menu has Install app, Send feedback and Sign out. Delete my account removes your username, profile and sign-in, deletes campaigns you own and takes you out of the others.

Reminders & notifications

Under Your account, choose whether to get a reminder the day before a session (by email, and as a notification where they're on), and turn on notifications for the device you're using. You can get one when the GM reveals a clue to you and, if you like, when it's the investigators' turn in a fight. On iPhone and iPad, notifications work in the installed app.

Arrange your campaigns

On My campaigns, press and hold a campaign card (or click and drag it on a computer) and drop it where you want it. Your order is saved to your account, so it's the same on every device.

Install it like an app

Choose Install app in the menu under your icon. On an iPhone or iPad, tap Safari’s Share button and then Add to Home Screen. The ledger then opens full-screen from its own icon. On iPhone you sign in once more inside the app.

Questions

Do I need the Core Rulebook?

Yes, to play (buy it from Asmodee). The site tracks your game; it doesn't replace the rules. Knack effects and other game text here are short summaries in our own words, not the book's text, and you can edit them on your sheet. See About this site.

I can't change something on a sheet.

Only an investigator's player can change it (plus the GM's session tools, and the owner if they've turned on Let me edit every investigator). Check who it says plays the investigator at the top of the sheet.

A banner says the daily limit was reached.

That shouldn't normally happen. If it does, changes won't save until it resets around midnight Pacific time; nothing already saved is lost. Keep track on paper until then and let us know with Send feedback.

It says I'm offline.

Your connection dropped. Changes you make are kept and sync when you're back online, as long as you leave the page open.

I didn't get the invite email.

Check your spam or junk folder for a message from arkhamrpgledger@gmail.com, and mark it Not spam so later ones arrive normally. Either way, the invite is waiting on your My campaigns page once you sign in with the address it was sent to.

I'm not getting notifications.

Open Your account and check that notifications say On for this device, then tap Send a test. On iPhone and iPad they only work in the installed app (iOS 16.4 or later), so install it first and turn them on from inside it. If the site says notifications are blocked, allow them in your browser's site settings or the phone's Settings › Notifications. While the app is open on screen you won't get a pop-up; new clues just appear in the Journal.

Something's broken or I have an idea.

Click your icon at the top right and choose Send feedback, or use Contact at the bottom of any page to send a message (no account needed).

  • You need the Core Rulebook. The site keeps track of your game; it doesn't teach or replace the rules. Please buy the book to play.
  • Game text is paraphrased. Knack effects, gear notes, enemy profiles and the reminders on the printable sheet are short summaries in our own words, not copied from the book. Names and numbers are only there so your sheet can track them.
