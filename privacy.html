Privacy policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Arkham Ledger is a free, hobby site for keeping Arkham Horror RPG character sheets and campaign notes in sync for a play group. It is run by one person, not a company. This page explains what it keeps about you and why.

What the site keeps

  • Your account. Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, are held by Firebase Authentication so you can sign in. The site itself stores only the username you choose.
  • Your campaigns. Campaign names and pictures, who belongs to each one and their role, investigator sheets, the Journal, house rules, and the GM's scenes, enemies, clues (including any pictures attached to them), notes and activity log.
  • Invites. When an owner invites someone by email, that email address is stored with the invite until it's accepted, declined or cancelled.
  • On your device. Your browser keeps a copy of recent campaign data so pages load quickly, plus your sign-in. Nothing is used for advertising or tracking, and there are no analytics.

Who can see it

  • Members of a campaign can see its investigator sheets, Journal and anything the GM has shown to players. The GM and the campaign owner can see the GM's private material.
  • Other signed-in users can see your username, for example on an invite. They can't see your email address.
  • The site's owner can see the stored data through the Firebase console, and only looks when fixing problems or handling a request from you.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Services the site uses

The site runs on Google Firebase (hosting, sign-in and the database), so your data is stored on Google's servers. Google also handles your IP address and browser details when you visit, as any web host does.

This site is protected by reCAPTCHA, which checks that visits come from a real browser rather than a bot. The Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

Removing your data

You can leave a campaign, give up an investigator, or (as an owner) remove investigators from inside the site. To delete your account and everything tied to it, or to ask what's stored about you, email maniac78@gmail.com. Requests are handled by hand, usually within a few days.

Investigators you made in someone else's campaign belong to that campaign's story, so the owner may keep the sheet after you leave unless you ask for it to be removed.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions go to maniac78@gmail.com.

Arkham Ledger is a fan project and isn't affiliated with Fantasy Flight Games.
